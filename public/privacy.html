Your camera stays yours.

Motion Arcade uses your camera to estimate hand or body landmarks in real time. Camera frames are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded or saved by the app.

No account. No ads. No third-party analytics.

The application code does not include an account system, advertising network, or analytics tracker.

Camera and microphone

Camera access begins only after you choose to set up or launch camera tracking and approve the browser permission prompt. You can stop the camera from Settings or revoke access in your browser at any time. Motion Arcade does not request microphone access.

Network access

Tracking models run on your device. Motion Arcade serves its pinned MediaPipe Hands and MoveNet body-pose model files from the same site as the app; it does not send model requests or camera images to a third-party tracking service. Your browser may still contact the site host to download application and model files when they are not already cached.

Information saved on this device

Motion Arcade may use browser storage (localStorage) for calibration, accessibility and control preferences, favorites and recent activities, gesture personalization, and selected game or lab progress. This data stays in this browser profile. You can remove it from Settings or by clearing this site's browser data.

Development logs

When a developer runs the local development server, diagnostic events may be written to the repository's local logs/ directory. Production builds do not send those logs. Diagnostic events can include browser details and app state, but the logger does not capture or serialize camera frames.

Questions or security reports

Review the source or report a concern in the project repository.
